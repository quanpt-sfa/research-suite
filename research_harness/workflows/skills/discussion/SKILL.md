# Discussion Workflow (Workflow 5)

## Purpose & Scientific Objective
Interpret empirical findings in light of prior literature, theoretical foundations, and methodological assumptions. Distinguish observation from inference, analyze boundary conditions, and revisit contribution statements if findings challenge prior hypotheses.

## Mandatory Invariants
1. **Distinguish Data from Inference:** Clearly delineate what the empirical results directly demonstrate from what the author conceptually infers.
2. **Do Not Merely Paraphrase Results:** Discussion is not a rewording of tables; it synthesizes theoretical meaning, mechanisms, and broader scholarly significance.
3. **Contradiction Transparency:** Explicitly address any empirical findings that contradict prior literature or the initial contribution hypotheses.
4. **Reopening Contribution Finding:** If an empirical finding invalidates or alters a core contribution claim, flag that Contribution Finding must be re-executed.

## Step-by-Step Task Instructions
1. **Analyze Results in Theoretical Context:** Relate observed coefficients, patterns, and mechanisms to the core theoretical framework.
2. **Formulate Theoretical Implications:** Explain how the findings modify, extend, or falsify existing theories.
3. **Assess Plausible Alternative Explanations:** Evaluate non-focal mechanisms or rival explanations that could account for the observed patterns.
4. **Contrast with Prior Literature:** Highlight where the results align with or contradict earlier published work.
5. **Delineate Boundary Conditions & Scope:** Specify limits of sample, time period, institutional setting, or measurement validity.
6. **Revise & Refine Contribution Statements:** Update contribution claims based on confirmed empirical evidence.
